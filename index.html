Why the alerts are worth trusting

Locked behind your Apple ID, not a password we invented

The blocking uses Family Controls, authorised in child mode. Your child can't delete the app, and there is no Shieldora screen that turns protection off. Lifting it means going into iOS Settings, where iOS asks for the parent's Apple Account password — not the child's passcode, not their Face ID. That is what makes the alerts worth trusting: protection can't be quietly removed first, and if anyone tries, that's an alert too.

You're the last to know

Something happens on a screen in another room and nothing reaches you. Most parents find out long after — if they find out at all.

FAQ

Questions parents ask

What exactly do the alerts tell me?

Behaviour, not content. That a blocked site was hit, or that protection was turned off or came back on, with the time and the device.

Do you see my child's browsing history or messages?

No. We don't collect search terms, typed text, messages or browsing history. That's a deliberate line, not a feature we haven't built yet.

Can my child just turn it off?

No. There is no Shieldora screen that turns protection off, and the app can't be deleted. Lifting it means going into iOS Settings, where iOS asks for the parent's Apple Account password — not the child's device passcode or Face ID. And if protection is ever switched off, you get an alert, so it can't happen quietly.

Will it catch everything?

No filter is perfect, and some content will get through. That is exactly why the alerts matter: you hear what was blocked and when protection changed, rather than assuming nothing ever got past.

How does the blocking actually work?

Shieldora uses filtering built into iOS, on your child's iPhone or iPad. The filtering happens on the device itself.

Which browsers does it cover?

Safari, including private browsing. Other browsers are blocked by default. You can allow one if your child needs it, and we'll warn you that content inside it isn't filtered.

When can I get it?

Shieldora isn't released yet. Join the waitlist and you'll be among the first families we let in.
